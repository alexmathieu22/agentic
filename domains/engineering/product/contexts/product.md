## Product conventions

- A requirement states the user's problem and the observable outcome. If it
  states a solution, the real requirement is still unwritten.
- Every story has acceptance criteria that someone other than the author could
  verify without asking a question.
- Scope is cut by removing whole outcomes, not by half-building several.
- "Nice to have" is not a priority. Either it's in this increment or it isn't.
- Record what was decided *and what was rejected*, so the same debate doesn't
  reopen in three weeks.
